Evaluating Commercial Replacement Cost Estimates: Beyond the Numbers Part 1

Source: www.insurancejournal.com · Score: 0.72
Why it matters: Discusses commercial replacement cost estimation, valuation accuracy, and underwriting risk—topics pertinent to Lloyd's property insurance market.
  • Commercial replacement cost software is an estimating tool, not a definitive valuation; its reliability depends on accurate building information, selected assumptions and professional judgement.
  • Inputs such as construction type, occupancy, size, configuration, quality, basements, fire protection, local construction costs, code requirements and debris removal can materially affect an estimate. Incorrect or overlooked information may result in an inadequate insurance limit.
  • Replacement cost is distinct from market value, original construction cost, tax assessments, depreciated book values, mortgage balances and purchase prices. It generally estimates the current cost of reconstructing a building with materials and construction of comparable kind and quality.
  • An inadequate limit may leave an insured with a substantial uninsured loss or a coinsurance penalty, and may lead to an errors and omissions claim against an agent. The article says estimates should be assessed critically, with a more detailed appraisal or professional valuation considered when warranted.
