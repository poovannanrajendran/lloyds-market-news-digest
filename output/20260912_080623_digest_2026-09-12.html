Digital footprint data can improve cyber claim prediction: Gallagher Re and KYND - Reinsurance News

Source: www.reinsurancene.ws · Score: 0.82
Why it matters: Discusses cyber underwriting insights and predictive indicators relevant to the Lloyd's market.
  • Gallagher Re and KYND analysis of 63,000+ insured organisations shows digital footprint (technographic) data improves prediction of cyber claim frequency beyond traditional firmographics (revenue, industry, geography).
  • Most predictive technographic indicators: distinct ISP count (strongest), email provider diversity, externally exposed services and IP footprint — reflecting the scale and complexity of an organisation’s internet-facing environment.
