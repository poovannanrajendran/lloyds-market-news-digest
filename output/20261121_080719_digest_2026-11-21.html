Four Lakes Re Ltd. (Series 2026-1)

Source: www.artemis.bm · Score: 0.80
Why it matters: Catastrophe bond (ILS) issuance and reinsurance structuring news relevant to Lloyd's market activity.
  • American Family Mutual Insurance Company is sponsoring Four Lakes Re Ltd. Series 2026-1, targeting at least $275 million of multi-peril US catastrophe reinsurance protection through three tranches.
  • The notes are intended to provide three years of cover, from January 2026 to December 2028, for American Family and its subsidiaries. Classes A and B cover named storm, earthquake, severe thunderstorm, winter storm and wildfire on an indemnity per-occurrence basis; Class C covers named storm and earthquake on an indemnity annual aggregate basis.
  • The currently sized tranches are $125 million for Class A, attaching at $2.9 billion; $50 million for Class B, attaching at $2.1 billion; and $100 million for Class C, attaching at $900 million, subject to a $50 million franchise deductible and a $600 million maximum event contribution cap.
  • Indicative risk interest spread guidance is 2.75%–3.25% for Class A, 3.75%–4.25% for Class B and 4.5%–5.25% for Class C.

Allianz Commercial warns AI-related insurance claims could mask greater financial exposure - Reinsurance News

Source: www.reinsurancene.ws · Score: 0.72
Why it matters: Allianz Commercial warns of AI-driven insurance exposures and risk management implications for the insurance market.
  • Allianz Commercial warns that AI-related losses may be substantially greater than confirmed insurance claims indicate. It says individual incidents could generate losses of around US$100 million, while shared technologies could expose multiple businesses to losses at the same time.
  • The insurer says AI-related losses may be recorded under existing covers, including cyber, product liability, crime and directors’ and officers’ insurance. Examples of emerging disputes include legal proceedings over allegedly faulty AI-powered vehicle safety systems and shareholder actions concerning disclosures about AI activities.
  • Allianz Commercial’s framework groups AI-related loss exposures into four types: harm directly caused by AI outputs or decisions; AI contributing to wider corporate or legal problems, including alleged AI-washing; AI increasing the effectiveness or scale of harmful activity; and dependence on AI worsening the effects of a disruption.
  • AI rose from tenth to second place in the Allianz Risk Barometer 2026, although it was not among the 20 largest sources of commercial insurance losses based on confirmed claims. MIT AI Risk Initiative figures cited in the report recorded 179 AI incidents in 2023, 307 in 2024 and 453 in 2025, an increase of more than 150% over two years.

Arbol introduces GPU value protection as AI infrastructure spending increases - Reinsurance News

Source: www.reinsurancene.ws · Score: 0.72
Why it matters: Introduces GPU residual value protection, a parametric insurance product for AI hardware risk.
  • Arbol has launched GPU Residual Value Protection for businesses and financial institutions exposed to declines in GPU values, including cloud operators, infrastructure owners, lessors, lenders and residual-value guarantors.
  • The product uses a GPU rental-price benchmark. At the end of the agreed coverage period, it pays a cash settlement if the index falls below a customer-selected threshold; policyholders do not need to prove an actual loss or obtain an asset valuation.
  • Customers set the protection period, payment trigger and maximum amount, with terms that can be aligned to leases, lending agreements or depreciation periods. GPU owners can continue using or leasing their hardware without selling it to qualify for payment.
  • Arbol said the product addresses uncertainty over GPU values as new processor generations arrive at roughly annual intervals, while some businesses depreciate hardware over five years or more.
