AI can cut reinsurance’s operational drag – Mea Platform

Source: insurancetimes.co.uk
Why it matters: This piece signals an actionable opportunity for Lloyd's market participants and global specialty brokers to convert soft-market pressure into a capability advantage by applying AI to manual reinsurance workflows. It matters to syndicates and placement platforms because automation can shorten lead times, lower placement costs, and improve data fidelity for pricing and claims. Executives should view AI not as a theoretical tool for modelling but as an operational priority requiring data engineering, platform partners and change management.
  • Execute focused AI pilots on high-volume mid/back-office tasks (e.g., bordereaux processing, treaty accounting, document extraction) with measurable KPIs: reduction in cycle time, error rate and FTE reallocation.
  • Build a canonical data model and API-first integration strategy across brokers, syndicates and placement platforms to ensure AI models operate on clean, interoperable data and accelerate straight-through processing for placements.
  • Establish governance: vendor due diligence, explainability standards, regulatory compliance checkpoints and a phased change plan to upskill staff and redeploy underwriting capacity toward complex specialty exposures
