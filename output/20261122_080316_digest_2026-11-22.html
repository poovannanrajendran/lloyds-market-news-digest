  • The proposed tranches total $475 million: two $100 million Class A tranches, two $100 million Class B tranches and a $75 million Class C tranche. Their stated attachment points range from $300 million to $1 billion, with indicative spreads from 2.75% to 8.5%.

Finding the Best Defense to Protect Against Cargo Theft

Source: www.insurancejournal.com · Score: 0.80
Why it matters: Discusses cargo theft trends and insurer underwriting changes affecting cargo insurance markets.
  • Cargo theft is creating pressure on insurers to increase rates and deductibles and add exclusions; some have removed theft protection from standard cargo policies and require separate cover.
  • Verisk CargoNet recorded 1,120 theft incidents and more than $121 million in estimated losses in the first five months of 2026. The article also reports estimated losses of $304.6 million in 2026 and a 26% year-on-year fall in incidents across the US and Canada.
  • Business email compromise is a primary access point for sophisticated theft schemes, but traditional physical theft remains the largest exposure, accounting for 70% of cargo theft, according to Scott Cornell.
  • Cornell recommends establishing theft-prevention procedures, training staff and vetting transport partners before relying on technology. He identifies drivers as the first line of defence against physical theft.
