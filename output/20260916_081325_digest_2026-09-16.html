Parametric insurance becoming a key tool to manage risk as temperatures rise: Swiss Re - Reinsurance News

Source: www.reinsurancene.ws · Score: 0.78
Why it matters: Covers insurance market responses to extreme heat (parametric heat insurance) and resilience strategies relevant to Lloyd's market news.
  • Extreme heat is becoming a chronic systemic risk—rising baseline temperatures are increasing mortality (an estimated ~489,000 deaths/year), stressing infrastructure, energy, transport and supply chains, and producing more frequent record‑breaking heat events.
  • Parametric heat insurance is emerging as a practical rapid‑response risk transfer, enabling quick, transparent payouts for affected communities, municipalities and businesses; pilots (e.g., SEWA covering ~50,000 self‑employed women and a US Southwest city programme) demonstrate reach to vulnerable populations.
  • Key buyer segments are governments, corporates, agriculture and livestock operations, energy and utilities, customer‑facing businesses, and nonprofit/development organisations—each using parametric structures to manage acute heat impacts.
  • Material limitations remain: parametric payouts indemnify exposure rather than prevent damage, simple triggers can misalign with complex human and infrastructure impacts (basis risk), and product design must reflect local vulnerability profiles.
