Viewpoint: Surplus Lines Insurers Offer Vital Cover for Growing Tech-Driven Industries

Source: www.insurancejournal.com · Score: 0.82
Why it matters: Covers surplus lines, GenAI, autonomous vehicles and life sciences risks—central topics in Lloyd's market coverage and specialty insurance.
  • Surplus lines are becoming the primary avenue for covering complex, tech-driven exposures because admitted markets often lack products or impose broad AI exclusions; surplus carriers’ freedom of form/rate and innovation make them suited to bespoke solutions for emerging risks.
  • GenAI exposures are pervasive across industries (bias, defective outputs, IP infringement, privacy/regulatory breaches, cyber) and present unique underwriting challenges: rapidly evolving models, scarce stable loss data, post-bind exposure changes, and multi-line allocation disputes—driving demand for affirmative standalone AI liability, specialized endorsements, and DUA/wholesale solutions in the non‑admitted market.
  • Autonomous vehicles create a new risk profile at the intersection of product liability, auto liability and software/cyber failures; limited actuarial comparables for driverless fleets complicate underwriting, pricing and risk transfer strategies.
  • Rising technological interconnectedness and globalized operations amplify systemic and cyber exposures, increasing need for specialized surplus lines programs and for brokers/insurers to conduct detailed assessments of clients’ AI/AV/life‑science technology use to place appropriate coverages.
