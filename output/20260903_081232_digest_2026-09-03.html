Lloyd’s reinsurance pricing softens, but AM Best sees adequate rates through 2026 - Reinsurance News

Source: www.reinsurancene.ws · Score: 0.85
Why it matters: Direct discussion of Lloyd's reinsurance segment, pricing trends and profitability outlook as assessed by AM Best.
  • Lloyd’s reinsurance is experiencing accelerated softening in 2026—most pronounced in property catastrophe (especially US‑exposed risks)—with broad declines in risk‑adjusted pricing and pressure on terms and conditions; rates have moderated from a very strong peak but are expected to remain adequate through end‑2026.
  • Reinsurance is Lloyd’s largest segment (about one‑third of gross written premium), generating £20bn of inward reinsurance premium in 2025 and growing at a 7% CAGR from 2021–2025.
  • Profitability for 2026 will be highly dependent on catastrophe experience (hurricane season ahead) and geopolitical developments; AM Best cautions a prolonged Middle East crisis could produce additional losses given Lloyd’s exposure and marine war‑risk activity.
  • Despite the softer market, Lloyd’s continues to attract capital—new syndicates and third‑party capital are growing, and the London Bridge risk transformation platform had deployed about $3bn by end‑2025 (including catastrophe bonds and reinsurance captives).
