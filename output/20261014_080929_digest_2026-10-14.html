Liberty Mutual Re completes parametric payout to Mexico following July earthquake - Reinsurance News

Why it matters: LM Reinsurance payout under Mexico's national parametric earthquake program; illustrates reinsurance usage and parametric risk transfer in a major disaster.
  • Liberty Mutual Reinsurance (LM Re) made a parametric insurance payout to Mexico’s federal government after a magnitude 7.3 earthquake off the country’s southern coast on 17 July 2026.
  • The earthquake triggered cover under Mexico’s nationwide earthquake and volcanic risk programme, which began just weeks earlier. LM Re has been its lead capacity provider since June.
