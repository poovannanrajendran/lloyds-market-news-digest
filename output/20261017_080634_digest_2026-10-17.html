Lloyd's issues guidance and expectations for broker-originated reinsurance structures - Artemis.bm

Source: www.artemis.bm · Score: 0.82
Why it matters: Lloyd's issued a market guidance/framework for broker-originated reinsurance structures, including governance, capital rules, and references to Aon-Blackstone/Cortina.
  • Lloyd’s has issued a framework for broker-originated reinsurance structures, effective from 1 January 2027, amid expected growth in demand for arrangements backed by external capital.
  • The framework requires alignment with Lloyd’s cross-cycle 95% net combined operating ratio target. Initially, no more than 10% of an individual broker’s treaty portfolio may be placed into these structures.
  • Brokers cannot own or capitalise the structures. Requirements include at least 19.99% member capital participation aligned with the managing agent, and at least 25% of capacity from lead reinsurers, with the panel agreed by the managing agent.
  • Lloyd’s sets requirements for governance, underwriting guardrails, portfolio oversight and eligibility. Annual plans and mid-year changes require approval; if indexation of at least 70% has not been reached by year three, Lloyd’s and the managing agent will agree next steps, which may include winding up the structure.
