Why it matters: Discusses alternative reinsurance capital and ILS trends affecting the reinsurance market, a key topic for Lloyd's market coverage.
  • Alternative reinsurance capital (ILS, catastrophe bonds and collateralised vehicles) has expanded since Hurricane Andrew — Bermuda became a hub for traditional capital, cat bonds grew from the early‑2000s and, since the mid‑2010s, sidecars and ground‑up insurance vehicles have increased market share.
  • Limits in the market are twofold: (1) traditional underwriting constraints — accurate modelling, pricing and declining P&C rates (still profitable but pressuring margins); and (2) frictions from the meeting of two worlds — financial investors and insurers have different contract mechanics, timeframes and transparency around claims versus financial payouts.

  • Outlook: alternative capital is likely to keep growing (today ~20% of reinsurance capital, Rousseau envisages up to ~1/3 over time). Reinsurers are expected to remain market leaders in risk selection/quoting, with financial investors becoming educated, aligned ‘followers’ — but continued learning from market events will be required.
