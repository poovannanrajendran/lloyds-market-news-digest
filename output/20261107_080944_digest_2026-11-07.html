Lloyd's syndicate Cortina launched by Aon and Blackstone funds with QBE as managing agent - Reinsurance News

Source: www.reinsurancene.ws · Score: 0.92
Why it matters: Launch of Cortina syndicate 1956 at Lloyd’s; major capital providers and QBE as managing agent.
  • Aon and Blackstone-managed funds have launched Cortina syndicate 1956 at Lloyd’s, with underwriting planned to begin on 1 January 2027 after receiving the Council’s in-principle approval on 16 October 2026.

Insurers urged to adapt reinsurance structures as secondary peril losses climb: Marsh Re - Artemis.bm

Source: www.artemis.bm · Score: 0.65
Why it matters: Discusses reinsurance strategies, catastrophe risk, and market dynamics impacting Lloyd's market participants, including modeling, exposure management, and ILS, though not mentioning Lloyd's explicitly.
  • Marsh Re says recurring secondary-peril losses, including severe convective storms, wildfire, winter storms and floods, are increasing pressure on insurers to reassess reinsurance strategies that have traditionally focused on rare, large catastrophes.
  • The broker reported that US insured severe convective storm losses have exceeded $30 billion annually since 2020, topping $40 billion in four of those years; the January 2025 Los Angeles wildfire was the largest insured wildfire loss on record.
  • Higher retentions following the 2023 reinsurance market reset have reduced the share of catastrophe claims absorbed by reinsurers, leaving primary insurers to bear more of the cost of frequent, smaller events. Marsh Re also found that model estimates can vary materially between releases, with uneven validation, particularly in less mature areas such as urban conflagration.
  • Marsh Re recommends combining vendor models with insurers’ claims experience, improving exposure-level mitigation data and reviewing cover for accumulated losses, including annual aggregate covers and second-event layers. It says access to reinsurance capacity will increasingly depend on the quality of insurers’ exposure assessments.
