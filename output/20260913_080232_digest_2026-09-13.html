Reinsurance offers strong diversification with total return potential: US Bank - Artemis.bm

Source: www.artemis.bm · Score: 0.75
Why it matters: Discusses reinsurance market opportunities, diversification benefits, and ILS instruments relevant to Lloyd's market coverage.
  • Reinsurance offers low correlation with stocks and bonds and relies on catastrophe-driven return drivers, making it an effective diversifier that can enhance long-term risk-adjusted portfolio returns.
  • Careful security selection and vehicles that diversify across peril types and geographies can mitigate single-event concentration risk, though tail losses cannot be fully eliminated.
  • Strong sector fundamentals support the case: U.S. insurers showed robust profitability (median annual earnings growth ~12% over the past 25 years) and reinsurance pricing flexibility aligns insurer and investor incentives.
  • Current market dynamics (as of October 2025) feature reinsurance income exceeding historical losses, creating a meaningful income buffer that enhances downside protection—while still carrying potential for periodic volatility and losses.

Casualty ILS inflows an incremental negative. Cat may soften more than RVS suggests: KBW - Artemis.bm

Source: www.artemis.bm · Score: 0.74
Why it matters: Market outlook on casualty/property reinsurance and ILS inflows affecting Lloyd's capacity/pricing.
  • Growing investor demand for casualty-focused ILS is likely to exert downward pressure on casualty reinsurance rates for several years — a negative for casualty reinsurers because a dollar of ILS capacity can support more casualty premium and the long-tail nature of casualty makes underwriting/pricing missteps slower to correct.
  • Reinsurers remain cautious on US casualty due to elevated social inflation, limited tort reform and tepid rate momentum; some market participants expect ILS inflows to be modest, but the relative size of the US commercial casualty market means even modest inflows can materially affect pricing.
  • On property catastrophe, most market participants expect ~10%+ rate decreases at the January 1, 2027 renewals (with larger declines in higher layers); KBW warns actual 1/1 pricing historically tends to be softer than Monte Carlo expectations, so downside risk to the ~10% figure is meaningful.
  • Sustained rate softening (casualty and property cat) will exacerbate underwriting margin pressure; disciplined reinsurers may under-deliver on premium growth, while brokers and asset managers with expanding ILS capabilities may see relatively stronger opportunities.

Ariel Re sees growing role for ILS in cyber exposure landscape: Carr - Artemis.bm

Source: www.artemis.bm · Score: 0.65
Why it matters: Covers cyber ILS and alternative capital, topics relevant to Lloyd's market dynamics.
  • Ariel Re is constructive on cyber ILS: as cyber portfolios grow and risk becomes more accumulation- and dependency-driven, alternative capital is expected to play an increasingly important role in cyber capacity.
  • Cyber ILS remains small but maturing — cyber catastrophe bonds account for ~1.3% of outstanding catastrophe bonds/ILS since the first cyber cat bond in 2023 — issuance has been subdued by strong traditional reinsurance competition and demand for nat-cat ILS.
  • Investor confidence and underlying cyber risk modeling are improving, and cyber is increasingly being placed in multi-peril catastrophe structures to diversify risk and broaden the investor base.
  • Because cyber exposures are dynamic and can share common failure points, continued market growth will require transparency, collaboration, cyber underwriting, aggregation and specialist reinsurance expertise — not displacement of that expertise.
