London Market signal: Impersonation and platform risks are colliding with tech-driven liabilities and accumulation pressures — time to tighten placement and underwriting controls.

Heads-up for brokers, syndicates and placement platforms: These items increase counterparty, distribution and reputational exposure and will influence capacity, wordings and ILS flows.

1) FCA warning on Sygnum Global - Why it matters: Unauthorised fintech/crypto providers in payment or escrow chains can leave premiums outside FSCS/FOS protection and raise settlement and fraud risk for syndicates.  
2) Gallagher Re launches a Digital Risk Practice - Why it matters: Formalised AI, data-centre and cyber capability will accelerate product and wording change and require brokers to supply richer digital-risk data to underwriters.  
3) Offshore wind (subsea cable aggregation) - Why it matters: A small component (subsea cables) drives repeat claims and creates serial-loss aggregation that forces rewrites of wordings, exposure data capture and reinsurance design.  
4) Allstate’s pre-tax cat losses hit $2.402bn (after July) - Why it matters: Material aggregate losses increase the risk of ILS programme erosion, pushing sponsors toward alternative capital or retrocession and affecting ILS pricing at renewals.
