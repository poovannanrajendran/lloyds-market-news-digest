The London Market’s growth story is increasingly about how capacity is organised—and how well firms manage emerging risks.

Today’s digest points to a bigger role for consortia, alongside sharper attention to cyber resilience and claims outcomes. These are practical issues for syndicate strategy, underwriting and operations.

1) Lloyd’s consortia could exceed £11bn in premiums by 2030 - Why it matters: Howden Re’s projection suggests consortia may become a more significant route for syndicate growth and capacity deployment.

2) Proposed UK cyber guidance for financial firms - Why it matters: Insurers and brokers may need to assess whether their incident response and recovery arrangements meet regulators’ expectations.

3) FCA and FOS call for better claims handling - Why it matters: Clear updates, timely progress and accountability for third parties are in focus, with almost 66,000 related complaints reaching the FOS over five years.

4) Up to 82% of data-centre disaster losses reportedly uninsured - Why it matters: The reported protection gap may prompt closer review of property and business-interruption cover for data-centre clients.
