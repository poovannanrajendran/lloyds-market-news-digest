London Market signal: Soft reinsurance pricing + abundant capital — discipline, clear wordings and stronger analytics will decide who keeps capacity.

Why it matters: Today’s digest shows the market can’t rely on price alone; syndicates, brokers and platforms need disciplined underwriting, transparent collateral and better portfolio analytics to attract ILS and protect ratings.

1) KBRA: Underwriting discipline in a soft reinsurance market - Why it matters: Soft catastrophe pricing only helps when carriers convert premium savings into strengthened protection, otherwise ratings and capital efficiency are at risk.
2) Beazley adds AI cover to cyber and tech E&O - Why it matters: New AI-specific extensions heighten aggregation and wording risks, forcing syndicates and brokers to define AI exposure and manage accumulation.
3) Allianz Partners to insure Waymo's European rollout - Why it matters: Autonomous mobility rollouts will require novel commercial motor and product liability structures and coordinated multinational placements.
4) Moody's launches Risk InvestorIQ for ILS evaluation - Why it matters: Improved analytics mean ILS investors will demand deeper portfolio-level scrutiny, pressuring syndicates and platforms to supply transparent loss-model inputs.
