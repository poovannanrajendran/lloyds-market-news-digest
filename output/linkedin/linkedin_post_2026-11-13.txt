January 1 renewals are shaping up around more than price.

Munich Re expects downward price pressure, while clients and brokers are also seeking changes to terms and conditions. For London Market teams, that makes contract detail, portfolio insight and risk selection worth watching alongside rates.

2) Delegated-authority data flows - Why it matters: Rising volumes and declining rates are increasing pressure on insurers and MGAs to manage processing costs and get quicker portfolio insight.
3) Accumulating catastrophe losses in Latin America and the Caribbean - Why it matters: Losses could erode retentions, earnings and capital, prompting reassessment of pricing and capacity.
4) AI adoption among London brokers - Why it matters: In a survey of 205 brokers, 96% viewed AI and emerging technologies favourably, while 69% expected significant or huge changes to the profession.
