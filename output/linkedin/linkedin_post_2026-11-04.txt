The London Market is showing both new capacity ambitions and signs of a more selective operating environment.

For brokers and underwriters, the useful signal is how expansion, returns and risk management are developing side by side.

1) Argenta expects Lloyd’s client returns above 18% in 2026, with profits narrowing in 2027 as rates soften - Why it matters: The forecast offers a forward-looking indication of how competition and softer rates could affect syndicate profitability.

2) Fidelis plans a Singapore Lloyd’s service company for treaty reinsurance - Why it matters: If approved, the business could extend Fidelis-backed capacity across Asia-Pacific from 1 January 2027.

3) Miller names Peter Stubbings CEO of Reinsurance and Gary Masters Chairman - Why it matters: The appointments could support continuity in Miller’s reinsurance and retrocession business, subject to regulatory approval.

4) Hiscox UK written premium rises 11.1% to £603.1m in the first nine months of 2026 - Why it matters: The reported growth provides a concrete trading signal, with digital direct and art and private client among the areas showing momentum.
