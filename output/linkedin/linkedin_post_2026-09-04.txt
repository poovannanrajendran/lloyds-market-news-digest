London Market signal: Pricing, underwriting and distribution are shifting — action needed on placement governance and product strategy.

This morning’s digest flags softer reinsurance pricing alongside improving Lloyd’s underwriting results, rising cyber distribution activity in Asia, and a clear distribution‑integrity risk from unauthorised online solicitations.

1) Lloyd’s reinsurance pricing softens in H1 - Why it matters: Softer reinsurance pricing reduces syndicate top‑line opportunity and increases broker leverage at renewals, requiring disciplined rate‑to‑risk decisions to protect long‑term returns.
2) Lloyd’s reports improved combined ratio and £1.9bn H1 underwriting profit - Why it matters: Underwriting discipline is restoring returns, but weaker profit before tax signals capital allocation should favour underwriting over reliance on investment income.
3) FCA warning: Reflex‑miner.com unauthorised solicitation - Why it matters: Unauthorised internet solicitations threaten distribution integrity and mean brokers, platforms and syndicates must tighten onboarding, screening and client‑fund controls.
4) Marsh–Resilience launch cyber product in Asia - Why it matters: New regional cyber capacity and distribution models can shift placement flows away from London unless syndicates adapt pricing, binding authorities and product structures.
