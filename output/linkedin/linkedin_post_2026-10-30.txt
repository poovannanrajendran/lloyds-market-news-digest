London Market renewals, catastrophe risk and new routes to cover: Today’s digest has several signals worth watching.

For brokers and underwriters, the stories touch on how renewal changes are communicated, how catastrophe losses compare with historical averages, and where reinsurance and protection are developing. These are signals, not confirmed shifts in pricing or capacity.

1) Lloyd’s renewal clauses and tracked changes - Why it matters: Underwriters cannot realistically review every clause at renewal, making clear visibility of policy changes important.
2) Aon’s $91bn estimate of insured catastrophe losses in the first nine months of 2026 - Why it matters: Losses were above the 21st-century average but below the five-year average, a useful benchmark for catastrophe-risk discussions.
3) Link Broker Europe’s entry into CEE and SEE facultative reinsurance - Why it matters: The Croatia-based brokerage adds a new specialist route for cedents in those markets.
4) AXA UK joining Pool Re’s SME Incentive Scheme - Why it matters: Selected digital SME products will include terrorism cover as standard for eligible businesses, potentially widening access to protection.
