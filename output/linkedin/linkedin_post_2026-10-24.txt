A clearer London Market signal today: New specialist capacity is in the pipeline, while softer property rates and complex risks put a premium on disciplined underwriting and strong claims capability.

Four developments worth noting:

1) Credeq’s specialist credit syndicate receives in-principle Lloyd’s approval - Why it matters: The proposed syndicate could add targeted credit, surety reinsurance and trade credit capacity, but still needs final permission before its expected 2027 start.
2) Global property insurance rates fall 10% in Q3 2026 - Why it matters: Marsh attributes falling rates and improved terms to ample capacity and lower reinsurance costs, increasing pressure to maintain underwriting discipline.
3) Cyber risk rises on board agendas - Why it matters: Willis reports that 70% of organisations formally include cyber in board-level risk frameworks, but gaps in directors’ understanding remain.
4) Property claims face delays and greater complexity - Why it matters: Labour shortages, equipment delays and increasingly complex losses could add pressure to claims timelines and loss-adjusting capacity.

Why it matters: Capacity, pricing, cyber governance and claims execution are all shifting at once.
