Competitive capacity is putting pressure on London Market pricing.

Today’s digest points to a market where property rate reductions are moderating, but competition across commercial P&C remains intense. These signals matter for renewal planning and capacity decisions, though they don’t determine outcomes for any individual placement.

1) Chubb’s warning on competitive capacity - Why it matters: Evan Greenberg said pricing is becoming more aggressive, particularly in the US and London, a potential pressure point for renewal discussions.
2) Property renewal rate reductions - Why it matters: CRC Group reported reductions easing from 13.3% in May to 11.1% in September, suggesting the pace of softening may be moderating.
3) Cover Re’s $65m capacity note - Why it matters: The reported note could add reinsurance capacity, although the excerpt gives no deployment details.
4) CyberCube and Blackpanda’s Asia partnership - Why it matters: An APAC regional view in broker cyber analytics may help inform comparisons across Asian markets.
