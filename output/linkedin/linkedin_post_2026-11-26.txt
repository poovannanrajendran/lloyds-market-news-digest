Lloyd’s capacity, catastrophe bonds and data-centre risk are all in focus in today’s digest.

For London Market teams, these stories point to shifts in capacity, risk financing and the exposures that may need closer underwriting attention.

1) Cedar Trace receives permission to underwrite through Lloyd’s Syndicate 1503 - Why it matters: The $300m syndicate is intended to add underwriting capacity and Lloyd’s distribution access for the 2027 Year of Account.
2) MS Amlin syndicate’s $150m catastrophe-bond debut - Why it matters: The reported transaction adds a specialty-market risk-financing development, though the excerpt gives no details on covered perils or structure.
3) Swiss Re warns data-centre growth could drive bigger, more complex claims - Why it matters: AI-related infrastructure investment may create a P&C opportunity alongside more challenging claims.
4) Hannover Re targets at least €2.9bn net income for 2027 - Why it matters: Its stated target, around 7% above its 2026 forecast, offers a signal of the reinsurer’s growth and profitability ambitions.
