January renewals, APAC growth and cyber accumulation are all worth watching across the London Market.

Today’s digest points to a property cat market where rate declines may slow, alongside changing sources of reinsurance capacity and evolving regional risks. These signals could shape renewal and underwriting discussions, though they are not confirmed market outcomes.

1) January 1 property cat renewals - Why it matters: RenRe expects rate declines to slow and continued discipline on terms, conditions and structures, informing cedents’ renewal planning.
2) APAC growth strategies - Why it matters: Gallagher Re reports 2025 growth averaged 4.4% across 14 markets, while AI, international expansion and plentiful reinsurance capital are shaping strategies.
3) French riots and SRCC exposure - Why it matters: Damage across multiple cities raises potential accumulation concerns for property insurers, with tighter reinsurance terms expected to leave direct writers carrying much of the burden.
4) Hacking incidents at South Korean financial firms - Why it matters: Reported unauthorised access highlights potential accumulation and systemic exposure for cyber insurers and financial-lines underwriters.
