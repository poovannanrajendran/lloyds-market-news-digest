Cat bonds and AI are both sharpening questions for the London Market: How risk is transferred, assessed and handled.

Today’s digest highlights four developments worth watching. They point to potential implications for reinsurance structures, MGA workflows and professional liability, but the reported outcomes remain unproven.

1) MS Amlin’s Kanata Re cat bond remains at $150m - Why it matters: The Lloyd’s transaction is expected to price towards the upper end of guidance, with Syndicate 2001 ceding and sub-syndicate 1673 also set to benefit.

2) SafePoint raises its Nature Coast Re 2026-4 target to $225m - Why it matters: The proposed named-storm layer would sit above $650m of recently secured cover, illustrating one approach to extending catastrophe bond protection.

3) AI-assisted professional work and negligence risk - Why it matters: Examples including hallucinated case law and flawed calculations highlight a potential liability concern where professionals rely on AI output without adequate checks.

4) mea Platform launches an AI underwriting solution - Why it matters: Its tools aim to assess submissions against carrier guidelines and coordinate workflows, though adoption and performance results have not been reported.
