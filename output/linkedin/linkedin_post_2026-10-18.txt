Alternative capital and new capacity are two clear London Market signals today.

Why it matters: Reinsurance structures, catastrophe bonds and specialist syndicates could all shape how risk and capacity are brought together. The detail and implementation still matter.

1) Lloyd’s guidance for broker-originated reinsurance structures - Why it matters: It sets expectations as demand for structures connecting capital with reinsurance risks is expected to grow.

2) Credeq Syndicate 1998 receives in-principle approval - Why it matters: Its planned focus on credit risk, surety reinsurance and European trade credit could add specialist capacity, subject to final approval and implementation.

3) SafePoint targets a $250m Nature Coast Re catastrophe bond - Why it matters: The proposed multi-year named-storm cover would extend across a wider range of US states than its previous sponsored cat bonds.

4) Aviva appoints Johan Slabbert to lead Global Corporate and Specialty and Aviva Syndicates - Why it matters: The appointment puts an experienced specialty-market leader at the helm of businesses relevant to Lloyd’s and the wider market.
