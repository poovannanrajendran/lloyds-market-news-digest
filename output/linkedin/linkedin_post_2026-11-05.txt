London Market signal: Lloyd’s is simplifying oversight while brokers look to expand access to reinsurance capacity.

For market participants, the changes could affect how syndicates are overseen and how eligible clients access capacity. The FCA’s quantum warning also puts technology resilience on the agenda.

1) Lloyd’s combines governance, performance, reserving and syndicate-level capital setting - Why it matters: A single oversight function could change how market performance and capital are managed.
2) Aon plans to increase Client Treaty co-insurance from 28.5% to 35% for 2027 - Why it matters: Eligible clients could access a larger co-insurance share, including on facultative reinsurance placements.
3) FCA warns quantum computing could undermine widely used cryptography - Why it matters: Insurers and brokers may need to consider potential risks to data, operations and transaction integrity in resilience planning.
4) HDI appoints Ashwin Kapoor and Tom Brown to its political violence and terrorism team - Why it matters: The appointments strengthen HDI Global UK and Ireland’s capabilities in these lines.
