London Market pricing is softening, while demand for reinsurance and investment in underwriting technology remain strong.

For London Market professionals, the contrast matters: Falling rates may affect renewal conversations, while capacity, accumulation and technology choices could shape how risks are placed and managed.

1) Specialty rates across London, Bermuda and US E&S - Why it matters: WTW says all 15 material re/insurance classes recorded net rate declines, with first-half reductions reaching up to 17% after claims and exposure trends.
2) APAC reinsurance capacity - Why it matters: Gallagher Re reports ample capacity and strong competition, which could give cedents scope to reconsider programme structures.
3) French riot-related SRCC exposure - Why it matters: Potential multi-city accumulation could leave direct writers carrying more of the burden if reinsurance terms tighten.
4) Guidewire’s proposed acquisition of hyperexponential - Why it matters: The deal, worth $1.7bn upfront and up to $2.1bn, signals major investment in AI-enabled commercial P&C pricing and underwriting tools.
