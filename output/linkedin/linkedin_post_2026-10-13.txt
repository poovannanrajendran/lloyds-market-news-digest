One clear London Market signal today: Specialist cover is being packaged around complex risks, while data and distribution remain under scrutiny.

Why it matters: Integrated placement could simplify cover for some clients, while better exposure data and careful oversight may help insurers assess emerging risks.

1) Price Forbes and Brit’s Accordium combines property, stock throughput and terrorism cover in one policy, targeting international risks with total insured values up to $1bn - Why it matters: It could offer brokers a more streamlined placement route for eligible SME and mid-market clients.

2) Verisk’s data-centre exposure work combines location, construction and contents data with catastrophe models - Why it matters: This could help underwriters assess concentrated property risks, while the discussion also stresses human oversight of AI.

3) SageSure has invested in Pillar, which manages more than $4bn in institutional assets and will remain independent - Why it matters: The partnership could connect SageSure’s underwriting expertise with a broader range of capital sources.

4) Oak Global’s Lloyd’s-approved portfolio underwriting unit will underwrite through Syndicate 1440, with risks set to incept from 1 January 2027 - Why it matters: It adds a route for distribution partners to access capacity across several specialty classes through one underwriting arrangement.
