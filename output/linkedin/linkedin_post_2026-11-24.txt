The clearest London Market signal today: Reinsurance absorbed around 72% of gross insurance-event losses in the PRA’s 2026 stress test.

For insurers and brokers, the findings put risk transfer and executable crisis plans in focus, while new third-party capital and specialist capacity point to continued activity across the market.

1) PRA stress test finds reinsurance reduced gross losses by around 72% - Why it matters: The result highlights reinsurance’s contribution to loss absorption across an exercise involving 25 major insurers, including the Society of Lloyd’s.
2) Atrium and CRC launch Fractal Re II - Why it matters: The portfolio is set to route property, specialty and casualty risks through Lloyd’s Syndicate 2026, backed by third-party capital.
3) PRA calls for regular reviews of crisis plans - Why it matters: Insurers are being asked to check that their response plans can be executed during severe events.
4) Russell Group expands specialty risk intelligence for corporate clients - Why it matters: The move could extend exposure-management capabilities developed across specialty classes to large corporates.
