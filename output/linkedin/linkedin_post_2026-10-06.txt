London Market signal: Pricing softness, cyber-physical losses and crypto counterparty risk are converging — immediate operational and underwriting action needed.

Why it matters: Today’s digest flags a likely near‑term reinsurance rate fall, high‑impact cyber incidents and an FCA crypto warning that together pressure syndicate margins, capital planning and vendor/onboarding controls.

1) Predicted up to 15% drop in reinsurance rates at 1/1 - Why it matters: Signals near‑term margin compression and forces syndicates to tighten underwriting discipline and reallocate capital.  
2) FCA warning on "Safe Wallet" (unauthorised crypto firms) - Why it matters: Brokers and syndicates using digital rails or tokenised capital face heightened AML, custody and reputational risk unless onboarding and vendor-authority checks are strengthened.  
3) Denmark data breach exposing 8.8 million people’s personal data - Why it matters: Creates large cyber‑liability, regulatory fine and class‑action exposure that will affect cyber pricing, limits and reinsurance layering.  
4) Hackers breached propulsion system of a US‑bound oil tanker - Why it matters: Escalates cyber‑physical loss scenarios for marine hull, cargo and P&I and requires updated wordings, aggregation controls and underwriting conditions.
