A busy day for specialty capacity: New European platforms are opening up specific routes into surety and transactional-risk business.

For London Market teams, the useful signal is where capacity and distribution are developing; these launches do not yet establish their scale or impact on placements.

1) MISSION’s French surety MGA, Trevanti - Why it matters: A specialist platform targeting fragmented European surety products and distribution could give brokers another route for this business.
2) Optio’s Amsterdam-based Pinnacle Specialty Insurance - Why it matters: The MGA plans to offer up to €20m for warranty and indemnity and tax liability cover, and up to €10m for contingent-risk cover across the EEA.
3) Link2Market’s Lloyd’s broker and coverholder launch - Why it matters: Its property and casualty binders could provide brokers direct access to capacity for risks that do not fit the traditional composite market.
4) WTW’s M&A activity figures - Why it matters: 614 completed deals worth at least $100m in the first nine months of 2026, up from 529 a year earlier, may support demand for transactional-risk cover.
