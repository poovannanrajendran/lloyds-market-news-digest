Price Forbes, Brit launch Accordium integrated property, cargo and terrorism facility

Source: www.globalreinsurance.com · Score: 0.92
Why it matters: References a London market facility led by a Lloyd’s insurer (Brit) offering integrated property, cargo and terrorism cover for international SMEs.

Verisk’s Newbold highlights data centre exposures and AI underwriting risks

Source: www.globalreinsurance.com · Score: 0.75
Why it matters: Covers data centre exposure risk, AI oversight in underwriting, and catastrophe modelling—central to Lloyd's market risk.
  • Verisk has developed a database of data centre locations, construction and contents, combining it with catastrophe models to help clients assess exposures. Rob Newbold said the US has roughly 2,500 data centres and suggested a single facility could generate a $20bn loss.
  • Newbold said suitable data centre sites with limited catastrophe exposure are becoming harder to find. He highlighted differing risks associated with coastal areas, fault lines and the wildland–urban interface.
  • Newbold warned that AI should not make underwriting decisions without human oversight, as repeated questions can produce different answers. Verisk uses AI in catastrophe modelling and to identify repeated exposure to the same property across reinsurance treaties.
  • Verisk’s average annual global modelled property catastrophe loss estimate is $171bn; Newbold said this is a mathematical average, not a forecast for a particular year.
