  • Insurers are tightening strikes, riots and civil commotion (SRCC) cover in some cases, using higher deductibles, lower limits, coverage caps or restrictions; some high-risk locations have lost cover altogether. Weather disaster losses account for higher share of national income in 13 countries, Munich Re finds - Reinsurance News

Source: www.reinsurancene.ws · Score: 0.72
Why it matters: Munich Re disaster loss study informs catastrophe risk exposure, insurability trends, and market implications for Lloyd's.
  • Munich Re compared weather-related disaster losses with gross national income in 20 selected countries over 1986–2025, averaging annual loss-to-GNI ratios over five-year periods.
  • In 13 countries, average annual weather-disaster losses as a share of GNI were higher in 2016–2025 than in 1986–1995: Australia, Austria, Brazil, Canada, France, Germany, Italy, Japan, New Zealand, Portugal, Spain, the United States and Türkiye.
  • Eight countries recorded their highest five-year average loss-to-GNI ratio in 2021–2025. The United States had the highest ratio among the countries studied, with losses averaging more than 0.5% of GNI per year.
  • Across the 20 countries combined, the share of recorded weather-disaster losses that went uninsured fell from roughly two-thirds in 1986–1990 to about half in 2021–2025. Munich Re said this aggregate does not indicate a uniform improvement in coverage or affordability, and around half of losses remained uninsured in the latest period.

Why it matters: Ruling on a health-club liability waiver affects liability insurance risk and coverage considerations for insurers, including market underwriters.
  • Massachusetts’ highest court ruled that a children’s gymnastics centre could not enforce a parent-signed waiver to block a negligence claim after a five-year-old broke her arm during a class.
  • The court found that the centre qualified as a health club under state law, which bars health club contracts from requiring customers to waive the right to sue over claims arising from activities there. The waiver and enrolment paperwork together constituted such a contract.
