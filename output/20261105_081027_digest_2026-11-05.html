Medicaid coverage losses drive up comp medical costs: WCRI - Business Insurance

Source: www.businessinsurance.com · Score: 0.75
Why it matters: Discusses Medicaid disenrollment as a driver of workers' compensation medical costs and its potential impact on market pricing/risk.
  • A Workers Compensation Research Institute study of data from 31 states found average medical payments at 12 months rose 3.1% for lost-time claims after the 2023–24 Medicaid disenrolment process.
  • Increases were higher for construction workers (10.1%) and manufacturing workers (6.1%). For lost-time claims overall, hospital payments rose 6.7%, while non-hospital payments fell 3.3%.
