Lloyd’s insurers may cancel cover over Hormuz toll payments - Business Insurance

Source: www.businessinsurance.com · Score: 0.86
Why it matters: Directly discusses a Lloyd's market insurance clause related to Hormuz toll payments.
  • Lloyd’s Market Association has published a clause enabling marine hull underwriters to cancel cover where insured vessels make payments to transit the Strait of Hormuz; such payments will be excluded and coverage may cease because of potential breaches of U.K., U.S. or EU sanctions or terrorism laws.
  • Immediate implications for insureds: vessels that pay “tolls” or levies in Hormuz face the risk of instant loss of hull cover, leading to heightened exposure during transit and an increased likelihood of claims disputes or denials.
  • Underwriting and wording impact: brokers and insurers need to review existing hull policies, voyage warranties and endorsements, update wordings to reflect the clause, and re‑price/underwrite exposures in affected trades (including war/sanctions risk).
  • Recommended actions: notify clients and brokers, strengthen sanctions compliance checks and declaration processes, consider alternative routing or contingency war-risk cover, and prepare claims-handling protocols for potential coverage disputes.

Source: www.reinsurancene.ws · Score: 0.85
