Insurance market must become more ‘risk aware and not risk off,’ says Lloyd’s Turk - Reinsurance News

Source: www.reinsurancene.ws · Score: 0.89
Why it matters: Direct quotes from a Lloyd's executive (Rachel Turk) on geopolitical risk and underwriting strategy.
  • Rachel Turk, Lloyd’s Chief of Performance & Strategy, said geopolitical and technological risks are interconnected, with events affecting supply chains, trade, energy infrastructure and insurance portfolios.
  • Turk urged underwriters to be geopolitically aware and “risk aware, not risk off”, arguing that insurers should seek ways to provide appropriate cover rather than simply exclude emerging risks such as AI. She stressed the need for clear contractual wording on war, AI, sanctions and systemic exposure.
  • She warned that fragmented cover for modern vulnerabilities could make insurers less relevant and lead clients to place risks in captives, which she said rarely return to the insurance market.
  • Turk set out five priorities: know what is covered; understand portfolio-wide exposures and accumulations; price for current conditions and maintain rate adequacy; adjust before results force action; and protect profitability to support future ambition.

Analysts view Cortina and similar structures as a negative for reinsurers amid market softening - Reinsurance News

Source: www.reinsurancene.ws · Score: 0.86
Why it matters: Discusses the Cortina syndicate at Lloyd's and its capital/pricing implications for the market.
  • Aon and funds associated with Blackstone launched Lloyd’s syndicate 1956, Cortina, which received in-principle approval on 16 October 2026 and is expected to begin underwriting on 1 January 2027.
  • Cortina will provide dedicated, multi-year reinsurance capacity using institutional capital and a conventional lead/follow approach. It is reported to bring $500 million of capital and write around $1.6 billion of premium.
  • Evercore ISI says additional capacity is negative for reinsurers in an already well-capitalised market and could put further pressure on reinsurance pricing, particularly if similar broker-led facilities emerge.
  • The analysts suggest these structures may be more disciplined than some past reinsurance start-ups. They expect Cortina to be a modest organic growth tailwind for Aon through volumes, market share and administration fees.
