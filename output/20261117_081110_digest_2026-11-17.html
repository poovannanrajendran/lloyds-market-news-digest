New California legal opinion sets guardrails for parametric insurance deployment: Ceres - Artemis.bm

Source: www.artemis.bm · Score: 0.65
Why it matters: Discusses parametric insurance regulation and adoption in California; relevant to insurance market developments that Lloyd's covers.
  • The California Department of Insurance issued a General Counsel opinion clarifying when parametric products qualify as insurance in the state’s admitted market: the buyer must have an insurable interest, the product must transfer risk, and the buyer must be able to show proof of loss.
  • Qualifying products are subject to Department oversight and the California Insurance Code, and to Proposition 103 where applicable, including prior rate approval. The guidance also calls for clear payout terms, objective and verifiable triggers, disclosure that payouts may differ from actual losses, and documentation, audit and appeal rights.
  • The opinion says parametric insurance can provide faster, flexible payouts and may reduce fraud through predetermined payments based on independently verified triggers. It should complement, rather than replace, traditional insurance.
  • Ceres welcomed the opinion and said it could support wider adoption in California. It cited existing parametric initiatives, including a community flood insurance pilot in Isleton and heat coverage for Los Angeles street vendors.
