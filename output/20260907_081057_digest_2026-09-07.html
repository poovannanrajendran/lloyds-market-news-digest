Sidecars steering towards casualty and specialty risks - Aon

Source: www.globalreinsurance.com · Score: 0.85
Why it matters: Mentions Lloyd's of London sidecar syndicate (Syndicate 1890) and ILS activity affecting Lloyd's market
  • Rapid expansion: sidecar capital reached about $23bn in H1 2026 (≈50% increase since end‑2024) as structures broaden from traditional property/CAT to whole‑account, casualty and specialty lines.
  • Structure and investor appeal: sidecars are typically 12‑month SPVs that let investors share premiums and losses alongside (re)insurers/MGAs; asset managers and private equity are driving demand by locking multiyear capacity at attractive pricing and enhancing returns via asset allocation.
  • Use cases and underwriting challenges: sidecars are increasingly deployed for MGA‑originated business and longer‑tail casualty/specialty risks where underwriting quality is harder to assess and losses can emerge years later, creating model, selection and tail‑risk issues.
  • Market outlook and caution: rating agencies and brokers expect continued demand (examples include Allianz/Oaktree’s Lloyd’s Syndicate 1890), but note these casualty and whole‑account uses are relatively new and need further testing around loss emergence and return sustainability.
