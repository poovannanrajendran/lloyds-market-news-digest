Iran war costs Lloyd’s of London $1.9B - Business Insurance

Source: www.businessinsurance.com · Score: 0.92
Why it matters: Direct report on Lloyd's of London's financial losses from the Iran war, a Lloyd's market risk event.
  • Lloyd’s of London took a £1.4 billion ($1.9 billion) hit from the Iran war, with losses driven mainly by damage to onshore infrastructure (reported Sept. 7, 2026).
