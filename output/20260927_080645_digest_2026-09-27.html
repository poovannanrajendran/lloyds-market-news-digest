Re/insurers unable to close nat cat protection gap alone, says Moody's - Reinsurance News

Source: www.reinsurancene.ws · Score: 0.79
Why it matters: Moody's nat-cat protection-gap report with P&C/reinsurance, risk transfer tools, and public-private financing—topics central to Lloyd's market.
  • Moody’s finds the nat-cat protection gap remains structurally large—more than 50% of losses are expected uninsured in 2025 and 57.8% of catastrophe losses since 2015 have been uninsured—underscoring that P&C insurers and reinsurers alone cannot close the gap.
  • Protection gaps vary materially by peril: earthquakes (~84% uninsured) and floods (~83%) have the largest gaps; hurricanes (~49%) and severe convective storms (~48%) are significant; wildfires are much smaller (~27%) because of higher take-up and lender requirements.
  • Key drivers are affordability and optional coverage, low take-up for infrequent/high-severity perils, insurers’ and reinsurers’ capital and return constraints (difficulty absorbing correlated tail risk), and industry cyclical pricing dynamics.
  • Mitigation levers for insurers and policymakers include boosting risk awareness, strengthening resilience of property/infrastructure, scaling risk-transfer (reinsurance, insurance-linked securities) and public–private financing, and exploring demand-side measures (pricing, mandatory or lender-driven cover) to increase penetration.
