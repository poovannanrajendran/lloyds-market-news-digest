27Lytes

Source: fca.org.uk
Why it matters: FCA warning on 27Lytes signals active targeting by unauthorised operators; such firms create counterparty, reputational and customer-protection risk for brokers, syndicates and placement platforms that may be referenced in distribution chains or used to launder premiums.
