Why it matters: An FCA warning that a firm is operating without authorisation signals heightened fraud risk to clients and distribution partners; unauthorised entities can siphon premiums, avoid regulatory protections, and create exposure for brokers and platforms that fail to detect them.
  • Immediate client and intermediary advisory: notify affected clients and distribution channels that the firm is unauthorised and reinforce that transactions with it carry no FSCS or Ombudsman protection.
