Executive summary

Lloyd’s is simplifying its operating model as Aon moves to expand pre-secured co-insurance capacity through its Client Treaty facility for 2027. In a softening market, Lancashire’s CEO is emphasising disciplined, active underwriting over passive capacity. Wider specialty-market signals include a leadership transition at the LMA, AXA XL’s commercial bonds appointment, and a UN disaster insurance programme backed by Gallagher Re and Liberty Mutual.
