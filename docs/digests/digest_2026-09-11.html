FxBlue Bridge (clone of FCA authorised firm)

Source: fca.org.uk
Why it matters: FxBlue Bridge is identified as a clone of an FCA‑authorised firm — a direct impersonation risk that can deceive underwriters, brokers and insureds, facilitating premium theft or fraudulent placements.
  • Market impact: Clone firms can divert premium, submit fraudulent documents/claims and impersonate legitimate brokers or coverholders, causing both financial loss and regulatory breach exposure.
  • Recommended action: Validate counterparties via firm reference numbers and direct confirmed contact details (not those on suspect emails/sites); require authenticated communications (corporate email verification, DMARC/SPF checks).
  • Risk indicators: Use of previously associated addresses, near‑identical names to authorised firms, unsolicited email contacts, and website differences (minor typos, alternative domains).
