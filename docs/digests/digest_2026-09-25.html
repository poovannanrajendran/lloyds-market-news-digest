Forward volatility: a model-free framework for hedging options risk - Risk.net

Source: risk.net
Why it matters: A model-free forward-volatility framework provides a market-extracted tool to isolate future volatility without relying on dynamic model assumptions. For Lloyd’s syndicates, brokers and placement platforms this can materially reduce model risk in pricing options-like instruments, improve reinsurance option valuation and inform capital-hedging strategies for volatility-sensitive lines.
  • Strategic: Use forward volatility as an independent market signal when pricing catastrophe options, ILWs and other contingent reinsurance features to reduce reliance on proprietary stochastic models and enhance comparability across counterparties.
  • Implementation: Integrate forward-volatility curves into valuation and risk systems (pricing, reserving and economic capital) and establish governance for periodic recalibration and liquidity assessment of implied hedges.
  • Risk/controls: Validate liquidity and execution costs for static hedges, document model-free assumptions for regulators and auditors, and run stress scenarios to capture basis and counterparty risk when hedging across maturities.
