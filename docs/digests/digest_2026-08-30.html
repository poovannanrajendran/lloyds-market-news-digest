Source: fca.org.uk
Why it matters: The FCA alert demonstrates how fraudsters clone authorised-firm identities to deceive consumers and counterparties; in the Lloyd's and global specialty context, the same techniques can be used to impersonate brokers, coverholders, or syndicates, enabling premium diversion, false placements, or fraudulent claims activity.

  • Integrate regulatory-warning feeds and regular checks of FCA/Warnlists into broker and platform onboarding workflows; require formal attestation from counterparties on communications channels and maintain an incident-response playbook for suspected clone/impersonation events.
