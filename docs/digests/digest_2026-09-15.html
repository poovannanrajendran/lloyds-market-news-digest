Source: fca.org.uk
Why it matters: The Upper Tribunal's upholding of an industry ban underscores intensified regulatory scrutiny on individual integrity and governance. For syndicates, managing agents and capital providers, this elevates the need for robust suitability assessments of investors, senior executives and influential stakeholders whose conduct can transmit reputational and regulatory risk across the specialty market.
  • Governance and capital due diligence: strengthen KYC/fitness checks on names, investors and capital providers, including behavioural and conduct assessments, escalation triggers and periodic revalidation of suitability for exposure to Lloyd's vehicles.

Premier Payment Solutions Ltd enters liquidation

Source: fca.org.uk
Why it matters: The liquidation of a payment services firm that supports agent networks and cross‑border remittances highlights concentration and operational risk in premium payment chains. Disruption to a PSP or its agent network can interrupt premium remittance, reconciliation and commission flows, exposing brokers, MGAs and syndicates to liquidity, client money and coverage risks.
  • Transactional remediation: conduct an immediate reconciliation of premium inflows tied to the PSP and its agents, suspend automated transfers where exposure is uncertain, and validate beneficiary accounts before any further remittance.
  • Commercial and platform resilience: require placement platforms and brokers to use regulated PSPs with contingency banking arrangements and escrow capabilities; mandate backup collection channels and periodic proof of client money segregation.
  • Legal and contingency planning: activate incident response playbooks, notify affected cedants and carriers, engage liquidators and legal counsel to assess recoveries and claims, and quantify short‑term liquidity implications for syndicate cashflow and solvency planning.
