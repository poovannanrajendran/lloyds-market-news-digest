HUOWEALTH

Source: fca.org.uk
Why it matters: FCA warning on an unauthorised entity underscores persistent marketplace fraud risks that can infiltrate broker networks and placement channels, exposing syndicates and platforms to reputational and financial contagion.
  • Risk: Unauthorized intermediaries can introduce non-compliant risk, leaving syndicates exposed to uninsured losses and client complaints when FSCS/ombudsman protections do not apply.
  • Action: Strengthen intermediary onboarding and periodic re‑verification (credentials, regulatory status, payment flows) before accepting placements.
  • Mitigation: Require documented proof of authorisation or formal refusal checks and include contract clauses to shift liability for fraud introduced by third‑party intermediaries.

Source: fca.org.uk
