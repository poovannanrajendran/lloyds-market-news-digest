Capital Zodiac (Clone of FCA Authorised firm)

Source: fca.org.uk
Why it matters: A clone firm alert published by the FCA signals active impersonation attempts that can specifically target brokers, Lloyd’s syndicates and digital placement platforms to misdirect communications, invoices and premiums. The specialty market’s reliance on trusted broking relationships and rapid placements makes it an attractive target for fraudsters using cloned identities and look‑alike domains.
  • Immediate containment: notify distribution partners, clients and internal front-office teams; flag and block the reported domains, telephone numbers and email addresses in corporate filters and AML systems; confirm all current placement and premium instructions with a secondary authenticated channel.
  • Operational controls: require two‑factor verification for any change to payment instructions or beneficiary details on placements and settlements; implement strict vendor and intermediary authentication for any new counterparty (including independent confirmation against FCA and other regulator warning lists).
  • Strategic mitigations: integrate clone‑firm intelligence into broker/syndicate onboarding and platform onboarding workflows; establish market-level alerting and rapid verification protocols across Lloyd’s brokers and platforms; consider escrow or trusted settlement rails for large/first-time premium flows to reduce diversion risk.
