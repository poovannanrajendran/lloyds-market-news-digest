Source: insurancetimes.co.uk
Why it matters: The report of organised, staged e‑bike and scooter collisions demonstrates a systematic fraud vector that increases frequency and complexity of motor liability claims relevant to Lloyd's syndicates, global specialty underwriters, brokers and placement platforms. The tactic exploits injured parties' vulnerability to coerce admissions and undermines conventional liability assessment, raising the need for coordinated market responses across underwriting, claims and placement operations.
  • Underwriting & portfolio management: Reassess appetite and pricing for micro‑mobility exposures; introduce targeted endorsements, stricter policy limits or exclusion schedules where necessary, and require telematics/CCTV/data capture as underwriting conditions to mitigate adverse selection.
  • Claims operations & anti‑fraud capability: Prioritise investment in digital forensics, rapid scene data capture, CCTV aggregation and cross‑market fraud intelligence; formalise specialist SIU pathways and fast‑track investigative protocols to preserve subrogation rights and limit indemnity leakages.
  • Brokers & placement platforms: Institute standardized evidentiary requirements at time of placement (timestamped footage, witness statements, device data) and ensure platforms support secure, auditable evidence exchange; syndicates should be selected for proven investigative support and willingness to apply robust declinature/subrogation strategies.
