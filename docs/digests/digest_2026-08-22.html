Source: fca.org.uk
Why it matters: FCA warning on Sygnum Global highlights unauthorised entities offering financial services; for Lloyd's market this signals risk where fintech or crypto-facing counterparties are used for payments, escrow or client onboarding.

  • Recommended mitigation: blocklisted vendor checks, require FCA registration proof, restrict use of unauthorised payment/crypto partners in placement workflows.
