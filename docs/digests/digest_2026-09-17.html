www.thinkingmega.com

Source: fca.org.uk
Why it matters: FCA warning that www.thinkingmega.com is operating without authorisation signals active fraud operations targeting UK customers; for Lloyd's market intermediaries this raises direct risks of clients being diverted to unauthorised providers and exposes brokers and syndicates to reputational and financial leakage.
  • Immediate action: verify client-facing links and marketing to ensure no inadvertent referrals; publish a short advisory to clients on verifying firms via the FCA register.
  • Payments control: enforce out-of-band verification for any new premium/invoice payment instructions and apply mandatory bank-account verification for large or unusual flows.
  • Brand protection: monitor domain registrations and marketplace listings; escalate takedown requests and notify compliance/legal teams to flag to FCA where impersonation of market participants is suspected.

Source: fca.org.uk

  • Legal & escalation: add a protocol to report suspected clone activity immediately to the FCA Warning List and to internal incident response for communication and remediation with affected counterparties.
